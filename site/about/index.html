A realistic horror job simulator

Echoes of BIC

You work the night front desk at a university's international student help desk. Most people are exactly who they say they are.

▶ Play the demo free Watch the trailer GitHub
In development. The demo runs in your browser: training plus the first two nights.

The job

Visitors come in with ordinary requests: a class schedule, a transcript, visa paperwork, a parcel from the mailroom. You check their form against their ID, look closely at their face, ask a question if something feels off, and make the call.

A few of them are not who they say they are. Nothing tells you when you get one wrong. You find out later, when something happens somewhere else in the building and the Dean comes down to your desk.

No monsters, no jump scares, nothing supernatural. The dread comes from people, paperwork, and an institution that is slow to take its own warnings seriously.

A real job first

Twenty kinds of request, a six-rule rulebook taped to the desk, a stamp, a rejection slip and a security radio. No menus.

Faces you have to read

Every ID photo is generated, and so is the person holding it. When they don't match, the difference is small on purpose.

Consequences that arrive late

Let the wrong person through and nothing happens. Not yet. The cost turns up a few visitors later, somewhere else.

Eight nights, five strikes

Strikes carry across the whole run. Three endings, depending on what got past you.

Watch how they answer

A glance at the exit, an answer that comes too fast, a silence that runs long. Innocent people get nervous too.
