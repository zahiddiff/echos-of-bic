## Name

Echoes of BIC

## Short description (under 300 characters)

Work the night front desk at a university's international student help desk. Check IDs and paperwork, read people's faces, and decide who gets through. Most people are exactly who they say they are. Nothing tells you when you get one wrong.

## About this game

Visitors come in with ordinary requests: a class schedule, a transcript, visa paperwork, a parcel from the mailroom. You pull up their form, compare it with their ID, look closely at their face, ask a follow-up question if something feels off, and make the call. Stamp it, fill in a rejection slip, or pick up the radio and call security.

A few of them are not who they say they are.

Nothing tells you when you get one wrong. You find out later, when something happens somewhere else in the building and the Dean comes down to your desk. Five mistakes like that across eight nights and you are done.

No monsters, no jump scares, nothing supernatural. The dread comes from people, paperwork, and an institution that is slow to take its own warnings seriously.

### Features

- **A real job first.** Twenty kinds of request, a six-rule rulebook taped to the desk, a stamp, a rejection slip and a security radio. No menus.
- **Faces you have to read.** Every ID photo is generated, and so is the person holding it. When they don't match, the difference is small on purpose.
- **Consequences that arrive late.** Let the wrong person through and nothing happens. Not yet.
- **Watch how they answer.** A glance at the exit, an answer that comes too fast, a silence that runs long. Innocent people get nervous too.
- **Eight nights, five strikes, three endings.** Strikes carry across the whole run.
- **A training shift** with your co-worker Rahat, one rule at a time.
